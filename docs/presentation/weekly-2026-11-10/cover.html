Tycho: a calibrated survey of training dynamics, measured with refined information

Question: which variables start to interact, at which order, at which training step?

The idea

Spectrometer: at one training step, sample the network’s variables (inputs, prediction, units); RI splits their dependence by interaction order.

Sky survey: measure thousands of runs (task, width, lr, data, weight decay, seeds) the same way. Cheap curves for all; RI for selected runs. One catalog.

Goal: regularities that hold across configurations.

The instrument: your RI
