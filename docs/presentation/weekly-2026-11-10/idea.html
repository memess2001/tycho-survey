The idea: one calibrated instrument, many training runs

LIKE A SPECTROMETER

Measure one network precisely

At a training step, sample the network’s variables (inputs, prediction, units). RI splits their dependence by interaction order. A significant subset is a “spectral line”.

Answers: which variables interact, at which order, from which step.

LIKE A SKY SURVEY

Measure thousands of runs the same way

Vary task, width, learning rate, data size, weight decay, seed. Cheap curves for every run; RI only for selected runs. One table: the catalog.

Goal: regularities that hold across configurations.
